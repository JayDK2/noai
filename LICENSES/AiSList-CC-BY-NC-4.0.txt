  Changes:    Yes. Changes were made. The two filter lists (aislist_blocklist.txt
              and aislist_warnlist.txt) are parsed from Adblock Plus format into
              JSON; each channel handle is percent-decoded and lowercased so that
              non-Latin handles match YouTube's own links; duplicates are removed;
              and any handle listed in mirror/retracted-youtube.csv is subtracted.
              The two tiers are kept separate and are NOT merged: the blocklist
              dims a video, the warnlist only marks it.
